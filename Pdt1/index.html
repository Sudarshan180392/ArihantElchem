Personal Care Packaging

Modern personal care packaging needs to deliver both high performance and premium aesthetics. Our coating solutions are suitable for metal packaging used for personal and cosmetic products.

Applications

  • Aerosol cans
  • Aluminium tubes
  • Monobloc cans
  • Cosmetic packaging
  • Personal & home care products
  • Pharmaceutical and specialty applications

Key Performance Benefits

  • Interior Protection: Barrier protection between the packaged product and the metal substrate.
  • Chemical Resistance: Coating systems designed for demanding product formulations and end-use conditions.
  • Durability: Protection against scratches, scuffing and abrasion during manufacturing, transportation and handling.
  • Premium Appearance: A wide range of colours, finishes and special effects can help brands create distinctive packaging.

PPG Asian Paints specifically describes its Personal Care portfolio for monobloc aerosols and tubes, with internal/external coatings providing protection, durability and decorative performance.

Food & General Line Packaging
Protection for demanding metal packaging applications

Food & General Line packaging requires coatings that provide a reliable barrier between the metal substrate and packaged contents, while maintaining flexibility, durability and manufacturing performance.

Applications

  • Food cans
  • General line cans
  • Metal containers
  • Specialty metal packaging
  • Steel & aluminium packaging
  • Can ends and related applications

Key Performance Benefits

  • Corrosion Protection: Helps protect the metal substrate from corrosion and interaction with the packaged contents.
  • Product Protection: Provides a protective barrier to help maintain the integrity and quality of the packaged product.
  • Chemical & Stain Resistance: Coating systems can be selected according to the chemical characteristics and requirements of the packaged product.
  • Flexibility & Durability: Designed to withstand forming, handling, transportation and other manufacturing operations.

PPG Asian Paints states that its food-can coating portfolio covers aluminium and steel cans and includes solutions designed for protection of contents, regulatory requirements, chemical resistance, flexibility and scratch resistance.
