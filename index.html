Who We Are

Delivering Quality. Building Long-Term Partnerships.
Arihant Elchem Solutions is a trusted supplier and distribution partner specializing in specialty chemicals, electroplating solutions, metal salts, and industrial raw materials for a wide range of industries across India.

With a strong focus on quality, technical expertise, and customer satisfaction, we provide reliable solutions to industries including electroplating, surface finishing, electronics, automotive, engineering, and metal treatment.

We are proud to be associated with Uyemura, a globally recognized leader in advanced surface finishing and electroplating technologies. This partnership strengthens our ability to deliver internationally trusted products and innovative solutions to the Indian market.

Our product portfolio includes high-quality nickel, tin, copper, zinc, and specialty chemical solutions sourced from reputed global manufacturers. Combined with dependable logistics and responsive service, we ensure consistent supply and reliable support for our customers.

At Arihant Elchem Solutions, we believe in building long-term relationships based on trust, transparency, and performance. Our experienced team works closely with customers to provide practical solutions, technical guidance, and products that meet evolving industrial requirements.

As industries continue to advance, we remain committed to introducing innovative technologies and delivering value-driven solutions that help our customers improve efficiency, quality, and competitiveness.
